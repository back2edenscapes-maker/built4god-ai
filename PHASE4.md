# Phase 4 — first customer mission

## Scope and actual state

Phase 3's storefront, private product, Wearebuilt4god live $9 checkout, account-free recovery codes, Scout, Marketer and verified revenue reporting are preserved. No new account, paid service, advertising, unsolicited message, live test purchase or refund was used. No external campaign was published. This phase narrows the existing segments and campaign library rather than creating another business platform.

## Buyer evidence and three campaigns

See [buyer evidence](marketing/phase4/buyer-evidence-and-channels.md) and [three segment campaigns](marketing/phase4/three-segment-campaigns.md). The strongest first test is a solo developer deciding what to investigate before a second build. Freelancers exploring a recurring client task and first-time sellers clarifying an offer are separate alternatives. Public first-person accounts establish that these problems occur; they do not establish demand for this kit, purchasing intent or market size. The archived SideProject discussion is research only and must not be revived for promotion.

Each campaign has useful original content, one question, a tagged preview URL, a seven-day review, and a publication gate. LinkedIn and YouTube official policies were reviewed. Their rules do not prove we have an eligible account or relevant audience. Reddit/Indie Hackers remain on hold when full local rules or AI policies could not be verified. HN and r/Entrepreneur remain excluded for generated content. All existing Phase 3 SEO articles remain drafts; no content is cross-posted automatically.

## Preview changes deployed

The preview now leads with a concrete action instead of a long introduction: choose an illustrative segment starting point or enter one buyer, task and difficulty. The visitor gets an interview question and can save a plain-text research note. The note labels the hypothesis as unvalidated and names the next permission-based research step. All typed entries stay in the browser/downloaded file; only an allowed aggregate note-save event can be sent. A note-save action is not proof of a completed download, demand or purchase.

The page explains exactly how the paid 11-file kit extends the free step, lists the six worksheets, preserves the $9 one-time price and links to the current purchase/support terms. `?segment=solo`, `freelance` or `launch` preselects an illustrative example. It is not segment attribution. Campaign source tags use the existing bounded channel categories; record segment and publication URL manually in the campaign register. Avoid concurrent same-channel segment tests with these limited analytics.

## Search discovery audit and repairs

Before editing, the homepage, preview, robots file and sitemap returned HTTP 200 without a public `noindex` header. Sitemap/robots infrastructure already existed and was retained. Homepage and preview lacked self-referencing canonical links and shared generic metadata. This phase adds separate titles, descriptions, canonical URLs and social metadata for home, preview and policies. Access/recovery/dashboard pages have explicit no-index metadata and remain out of the sitemap. Robots now allows their HTML to be fetched so crawlers can observe that no-index instruction; API paths remain disallowed. Access is still enforced by server credentials and payment checks, not robots rules.

Relevant official guidance: [Google JavaScript SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics), [canonicals](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls), [robots metadata](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots-meta-tag). Canonical signals are hints, not guaranteed indexing. A site-restricted search returned no results during this review. That is a discovery observation, not definitive proof of exclusion. Search Console ownership, indexing status, crawl history, actual search impressions and keyword volumes are unverified. No account or verification token was invented or installed.

## Weekly scorecard

The existing authenticated growth endpoint now includes a Monday-to-Monday UTC weekly aggregate. The existing daily growth workflow writes `output/growth/weekly-scorecard.json` and `.md`, so there is no additional scheduler or analytics vendor. Weekly metrics include storefront pageviews, preview pageviews, note-save actions, checkout starts, verified live orders paid in that week, gross subtotal and current refunds applied to that order cohort. Refund totals are not refund-date cash flow. Fees/net after fees remain unavailable. Zero denominators produce unavailable ratios. The collection start and partial coverage are explicit. QA, bots, repeat loads and opt-outs limit interpretation; these are event counts, not unique visitors or qualified buyers.

Voluntary, de-identified learning belongs in [feedback-log.csv](marketing/phase4/feedback-log.csv). Do not store names, emails, private client files, checkout references or recovery codes there. No feedback entries or publications were fabricated.

No system change in this phase can guarantee those outcomes. Publication approval must specify account, content and placement. The working payment and recovery paths stay available while those decisions are pending.
