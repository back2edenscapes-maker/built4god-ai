# Workflow planning toolkit

Draft for human review.

1. Define one buyer and one recurring task.
2. Record the current process in tracker.csv.
3. Measure time and errors before changing it.
4. Test one improvement with a small sample.
5. Record evidence, owner, due date, and next action.
6. Review results weekly and retain only useful changes.

## Buyer interview
- What happened the last time you did this task?
- What did the problem cost in time?
- What have you already tried?
- Would this workbook solve that specific problem?

## Publication checklist
- Customize with niche-specific examples.
- Test every instruction and open the CSV.
- Confirm all content is original or licensed.
- Validate demand and check selling platform rules.
- State exactly what buyers receive, price, support and refund terms.
