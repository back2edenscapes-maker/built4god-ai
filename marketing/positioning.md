# Three positioning options

Drafts for owner approval. Features checked against the v1 ZIP on 2026-10-18. No efficacy or revenue claim is validated.

## A — Decide what to build next

Recommended initial audience: independent software developers with one side-project idea and limited time.

**Headline:** Before you build your next side project, write down the evidence.

**Subhead:** LaunchReady gives you a practical workbook and six editable CSV worksheets to investigate a buyer problem, compare alternatives, and make a documented launch decision.

**CTA:** See the free preview. Secondary: Get the 11-file kit — $9 once.

Demonstrate replacing “AI tools for everyone” with one buyer, one task, and one unanswered research question. Hypothesis: decision structure helps this buyer. Seek specific feedback about a current decision, preview activity, and ultimately a verified purchase.

## B — Explore a repeated client task

Audience: freelance developers considering a small tool based on a repeated client workflow.

**Headline:** A repeated client problem deserves a clear test before a full build.

**Subhead:** Organize buyer conversations, existing workarounds, scope, pricing assumptions, and launch checks in one downloadable kit.

**CTA:** Explore the client CSV review example.

Demonstrate the hypothetical client CSV checker planning exercise. Label every invented entry illustrative. The kit does not contain the checker software. Hypothesis: freelancers recognize the workflow; verify through opt-in accounts of recent tasks and a need for documentation tools.

## C — Check the first offer

Audience: first-time software-product sellers with a prototype but incomplete listing and purchase checks.

**Headline:** Make the offer clear before you ask someone to pay.

**Subhead:** Use LaunchReady’s listing worksheet and launch checklist to document what buyers get, what is excluded, and what you still need to test.

**CTA:** Preview the launch decision process. Secondary: Get LaunchReady — $9 USD, one time.

Demonstrate checks for sample, compatibility, price, support terms, payment and delivery. Hypothesis: a checklist resolves launch uncertainty. Seek evidence that users identify a missing check and can use the sheet without explanation. This educational kit does not certify launch readiness.

## Experiment

Start A on one approved placement. Keep price and files fixed. Record dates, public URL, source tag, buyer-fit feedback, measured visits, preview interactions, checkout clicks, verified orders and refunds. Unknown values remain unknown. Review after seven days or five substantive opt-in conversations, whichever is later. This is an operating checkpoint, not statistical validation. Do not pick a winner from a handful of anonymous visits.
