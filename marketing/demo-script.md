# Product demonstration script — about 100 seconds

Draft requiring approval before recording or publication. Capture the real workbook and one sanitized worksheet. Do not show the complete paid kit, private delivery URL, order references, account screens, or customer data. Add readable captions. All demo entries are fictional and visibly labeled illustrative.

**0–12 seconds:** Show the public preview. “Before committing weeks to a side project, define what you still need to learn. Here is how LaunchReady organizes that decision.”

**12–30:** Show one fictional scorecard row. “Our illustrative buyer is a freelance developer reviewing a client CSV. Frequency, current workaround, and time cost are unknown. We leave them unknown instead of calling a guess research.”

**30–45:** Briefly show interview prompts. “Ask about the last time the task happened. Walk through the steps before presenting a solution. Record evidence privately and get permission for follow-up.”

**45–62:** Show alternatives headers. “Compare existing tools and manual work. If a free option already solves the task well, stopping or narrowing can be the useful outcome.”

**62–78:** Show a checklist excerpt. “Before selling, check the sample, listing, payment confirmation, and delivery. Practical checks are not proof of demand.”

**78–100:** Return to the product page. “LaunchReady includes a workbook, six editable CSV worksheets, an interview script, a listing template, and a license: eleven files, nine dollars once. No hosted app, leads, or income guarantee. Try the free preview and decide whether the structure fits your next software project.”
