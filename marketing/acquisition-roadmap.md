# Thirty-day acquisition roadmap

Prepared 2026-10-18. Relative days; all external publication requires owner approval. No ad spend, paid directories, unnecessary new accounts, affiliate payouts, or unsolicited bulk contact. Existing audiences and owner availability are unknown.

## Days 1–7 — Buyer fit

Run seven-day-launch.md for independent software builders deciding what to build. Use one approved owned-channel placement and, if approved, a recorded demo. Keep the $9 price fixed. Capture anonymous questions and objections. If viewers cannot explain what the kit contains, fix that explanation before adding channels.

## Days 8–14 — Searchable educational material

Approve article 1 for the owned site, linking to a relevant preview. Review article 2 against actual delivery behavior before approval. Both offer useful steps without purchase. No keyword volume has been verified. Google says search changes can take hours to months; do not judge SEO from a week without orders. Use descriptive metadata and crawlable internal links. [Google SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), checked 2026-10-18.

Consider a single SideProject demonstration after checking local rules. Research Indie Hackers permission; an inaccessible rules page means hold, not permission.

## Days 15–21 — Refine from evidence

Compare segments using consistent event definitions. Try positioning B only if feedback supports a freelancer workflow. Do not promote in r/freelance until local rules are verified. An existing consenting peer or educator might assess whether the free preview helps their audience. Any specific outreach needs owner approval; no commission program is implemented or promised.

## Days 22–30 — Choose a narrow next step

Prepare an original Pinterest checklist only if an existing account and relevant audience are confirmed. Product Hunt is secondary: novelty and fit for this template kit remain uncertain. Do not use Show HN for a reading/template bundle. Review approved placements and retire duplicative drafts. Choose the next buyer problem from specific evidence and verified transactions, not nominal channel scores.

## Decision rules

- Broken checkout/delivery: pause new promotion, preserve evidence, repair and verify without unauthorized live payments.
- Preview activity with unclear value: revise one explanation or screenshot and record the date.
- Clicks without orders: inspect payment path and consent-based feedback. Do not automatically discount or invent urgency.
- Paid order: privately verify payment, fulfillment and support outcome. Publish only aggregate metrics. Request feedback only with consent after time to use the kit.
- Refund/support friction: prioritize clarity and delivery before more traffic.
- Insufficient evidence: record the limit and continue focused research or stop.

## Measurement

Review health daily and campaign decisions weekly. State analytics collection start, status, and window. Checkout clicks / measured storefront views is an event ratio when the denominator is positive. Orders / views is directional, not a person-level funnel or causal source attribution. Keep gross product revenue, refunds, and known processor fees separate. Unknown fees mean net after fees is unavailable. Traffic, qualitative feedback, and purchases are different evidence.
