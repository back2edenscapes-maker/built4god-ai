# Seven-day organic launch plan

Start Day 1 after owner approval of the channel and asset. Days are relative so approval delays do not trigger stale publication. Budget: $0. Suggested owner time: 30–60 minutes/day, with a longer demo session. No third-party publication or outreach is authorized by this file.

| Day | Work | Gate and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review positioning A and preview with two already consenting peers, if available. Record comprehension questions. | Owner selects channel and wording. No eligible contacts means pending research; do not substitute cold messaging. |
| 2 | Approve and manually publish social draft 1 on one existing owned account whose rules permit it. | Record public URL/time/source tag; check analytics status before interpreting numbers. |
| 3 | Answer genuine questions, ask consent before private follow-up, review mobile preview. | Log objections and broken paths; likes are not demand. |
| 4 | Record the sanitized demo; review video, captions and description. | Explicit owner approval before upload to an existing channel. |
| 5 | Consider one SideProject placement only after a human checks current rules, AI policy and account eligibility. Otherwise choose a useful owned-channel post. | Owner approval and truthful commercial disclosure; no repost flood. |
| 6 | Review measured views, preview events, checkout clicks, verified orders and refunds. Ask consenting viewers what was unclear. | Missing data stays unknown. Check checkout health without purchasing. |
| 7 | Decide continue, revise or pause this positioning. | Document feedback and missing evidence. Count sales only from verified live reports. |

Five substantive opt-in conversations is a possible research workload target, not an existing audience, traffic forecast or statistical proof. If relevant people do not engage, improve buyer/channel fit before increasing frequency.
